# Vagrant Story Integrated Randomizer v0.1b

First public beta. An on-disc randomizer for Vagrant Story (USA): you pick a seed and options
in a menu at boot, and the game is randomized as you play, on the console itself. There is no
PC tool to run. Randomization rules are ported from ChaoticBrave's
[VagrantStoryRandomizer](https://github.com/ChaoticBrave/VagrantStoryRandomizer), and the
in-game menu is modeled on MottZilla's SotN Integrated Randomizer.

> **Note:** This project was developed with the help of AI tools, under human direction and
> play-testing.

## Downloads

Pick the patch that matches the game image you want to play. Each one only works on that exact
image.

| File | Apply to |
|---|---|
| `VSIntegratedRando-v0.1b.ppf` | Vagrant Story (USA), or Vagrant Story (USA) + Rood Reverse QoL mod v3.1 |
| `VSIntegratedRando-Zenith-v0.1b.ppf` | Vagrant Story Zenith 1.94 |
| `VSIntegratedRando-Zenith-QoL-v0.1b.ppf` | Vagrant Story Zenith 1.94 + Rood Reverse QoL mod v3.1 for Zenith 1.94 |

## How to patch

1. Start from a clean Vagrant Story (USA) BIN (single track, SHA-1
   `38c63db6c49a06b91d87cd5a4e31b1ec68fe212c`). Work on a copy.
2. If you want the other mods, apply them first, in this order:
   - Zenith: `zenith_194.ppf`.
   - Rood Reverse QoL mod: its xdelta. Use the regular version for the standard patch, or the
     "for Zenith 1.94" version after Zenith. Use the official xdelta3 or a patcher built on it,
     such as Delta Patcher.
3. Apply the matching randomizer PPF with any PPF 3.0 patcher (PPF-O-Matic, ppfdev, etc.).
4. Update your `.cue` file's `FILE` line if you renamed the BIN, then boot it.

## What's in this release

**Boot menu**
- Enter a seed, start with a random one, pick a preset (Standard, Chaos, Vanilla), or choose
  every option yourself. X confirms, O goes back.
- Save your seed and options to memory card slot 1 before starting, and load them again later
  with Load Seed MemCard.
- Your seed and options travel with your game saves, including New Game+. The in-game main
  menu (Triangle) shows the seed and preset.

**Chests**: random consumables and grimoires; random real weapons, armor, shields, accessories
and gems; random DP/PP, gem stats, class, affinity and blunt/edged/piercing values; random blade
cost stat; balance options for amounts, materials and stat ranges.

**Enemies**: random stats with story-based balancing; random chance-based drops; random
equipment and equipment stats; random body part HP, defenses, affinities and BEP; optional
Ice Wolf (City Walls Hellhounds breathe ice).

**Ashley**: random starting stats, weapon, armor and items; nine weapon builds plus Random;
random class, affinity and BEP for his gear and body; balance option with a Low/Medium/High
stat threshold; random Fandango cost stat.

**Abilities**: random Break Art costs and learning kills; random battle ability learning,
risk costs and timing windows; Lv.4 elemental spells can become the ultimate spells.

**Keys and progress**: Open World (start with every key and sigil); Iron Maiden Required
(Great Cathedral needs the Tigertail Sigil, and Kali drops the Gold and Platinum Keys); random
loot can also contain keys and sigils.

**Zenith features** (standard patch only): Perfect Analyze, friendly Manabreaker, boss Warlock
Grimoires, quiet Gremlins and Crimson Blades, free teleport, and repeat-kill drop rates.

See the README for a description of every option.

## Zenith edition differences

The two Zenith patches leave out the Zenith section, since Zenith provides those features
itself. Zenith also rearranges the game's ability data, so in the Zenith edition:

- Break Arts randomizes only the kills needed to learn each Break Art, not their HP costs.
- Ability Risk Costs and Ultimate Spells are not available.

Everything else works as in the standard patch, on top of Zenith's own changes.

## Known limitations

This is a beta. What has been tested so far: new games on all three patches in an emulator
(DuckStation), randomized chests, enemies, Ashley and abilities, saving and loading, and seeds
saved to and loaded from the memory card. Not yet tested:

- Real PlayStation hardware and optical drive emulators.
- New Game+.
- A full playthrough. In particular, the Iron Maiden Required route (Kali's keys, the Iron
  Maiden, Asura's Tigertail Sigil, Great Cathedral) and Ice Wolf have not been played yet.
- Long Zenith runs.

Bug reports with the seed, preset or options, and where it happened are very welcome.
